<Callout type="important">
This is a **pilot in seven councils only**. It does not change the law anywhere else, and it is
funded to 2029.
</Callout>

## What it is

From April 2026, seven English councils are paying eligible kinship carers an allowance equal to
the full fostering National Minimum Allowance, with **no means test**.

## The seven councils

Bexley · Bolton · Newcastle upon Tyne · North East Lincolnshire · Medway · Thurrock · Wiltshire

Councils that were already paying an NMA-equivalent allowance were not eligible to apply, so the
absence of a council from this list does not mean it pays less.

I re-check this list every three months, because a pilot is exactly the sort of thing that changes
quietly. It is what I hold today, not a promise about next year.

## Who qualifies

- You hold a special guardianship order, or a "lives with" child arrangements order.
- Without you, the child would otherwise be in local authority care.
- You are **not** already receiving regulation 6 financial support for that child.

## If you are in a pilot area but already on support

You cannot have both. But the comparison is still useful: if the pilot rate for your child's age
band is higher than what you receive, that is a concrete figure to put to your council when you ask
for a review. My calculator shows you the comparison when it applies.

## The caveats worth holding on to

- It is a pilot, running to 2029, and it is being evaluated.
- It does not change any council's legal obligations outside the seven areas.
- A national rollout would be a significant change, but nobody can promise one.

## What you can check, and what you have to ask for

You can check the facts of the pilot. The seven councils are named above, the rate is the fostering
national minimum allowance, and there is no means test.

What you cannot check is whether you personally qualify. That turns on whether the child would
otherwise be in care and on what support you already receive, and only your council can apply those
tests to your case. Ask it directly.
