If you do one thing after using the calculator, make it this.

## Why it is the first step

Everything else follows from the policy:

- It tells you what starting figure your council uses, and whether it is [the fostering
  allowance](/guidance/what-your-council-must-consider) or something lower.
- It tells you [how the means test is applied](/guidance/how-the-means-test-works), and in what
  order.
- It tells you whether [Child Benefit is deducted](/guidance/child-benefit-deduction).
- It is the document any later complaint is measured against.

A conversation can be remembered differently by two people. A policy cannot.

## What to ask for

1. Your current policy on financial support for special guardians.
2. How the starting figure for an allowance is decided.
3. How any means test is applied, and what income is taken into account.
4. Whether Child Benefit is taken into account.
5. How allowances are reviewed each year.

A link is fine if it is on the website.

## I may not hold your council's policy

My calculator uses the national minimum figures for your area unless I hold a **sourced and dated
copy** of your council's own published policy. I deliberately do not use policy details I cannot
point at a live public source for, naming a council and describing what it does requires being able
to show where that came from.

There is a duty behind this. Since October 2024 every council in England has had to publish a
**kinship local offer** setting out the support it provides. So a request for the written policy is
not a favour you are asking.

<Cite id="kinship-care-guidance-2024" />

So if the result said I do not have your council's policy, that is not a gap in your council's
duties. It is a gap in mine, and asking them directly is the fastest way round it.

## What you can check, and what you have to ask for

Half of this you can do without asking anybody. Since October 2024 every council in England has had
to publish a kinship local offer, so go and find your council's.

Its financial support policy is a different matter. That may not be published at all, and you will
not know until you ask. Everything else on this site leans on that document, so ask early.
