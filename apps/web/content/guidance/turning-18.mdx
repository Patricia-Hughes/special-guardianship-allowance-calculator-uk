Special guardianship orders end when the young person turns 18, and financial support normally ends
with them.

<Cite id="sg-regs-2005-reg9" />

## The exception

Where the young person remains in full-time education or training, support can continue. This is
not automatic and it is not always offered — it often has to be asked for.

<Callout type="important">
Ask **before** the eighteenth birthday, not after. Several months before is not too early.
</Callout>

## What to ask

1. What happens to my support on my child's eighteenth birthday?
2. If they stay in education or training, will it continue, and for how long?
3. What do you need from me, and by when?

Ask for the answer in writing. It is the document you will need if payments stop unexpectedly.

## Other things that change at 18

The young person may be able to claim benefits in their own right, and your own Child Benefit and
Universal Credit will be affected. This is a good moment to get a full benefits check from an
adviser — the changes interact, and a check often finds something.
