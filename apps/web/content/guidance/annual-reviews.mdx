Financial support is subject to review. In practice, this is one of the easiest things to let slip
and one of the easiest to put right.

<Cite id="sg-regs-2005-reg10-11" />

## What a review should involve

- The council asks you for up-to-date information about your circumstances.
- It works out whether the support should change.
- It tells you what it proposes, **before** it changes anything.
- It gives you a reasonable chance to comment.
- It tells you the outcome, with reasons.

## Why it matters more than it sounds

Rates change on **1 April** every year:

<RateTable nation="england" />

An allowance that has not been reviewed in several years has quietly lost value against those
figures. Nothing dramatic has to have gone wrong for you to be materially underpaid. It is enough
for nobody to have looked.

## Asking for one

You do not need a reason beyond "it has been more than a year". Ask in writing, and ask what
information the council needs from you so it can get on with it.
