Regulation 6 allows financial support to take account of a child's disability, and of the
consequences of any abuse or neglect they experienced.

<Cite id="sg-regs-2005-reg6" />

## Why our calculator adds nothing for this

<Callout type="important">
There is **no national figure** for an enhancement. It varies entirely by council.
</Callout>

So we add nothing, and we tell you to ask instead. Applying an invented percentage would produce a
figure that looks authoritative and is not, and if you took it to your council, the first thing
that would happen is that it would be dismissed, along with everything else you said.

## What helps a request succeed

Be specific and put costs against things. A social worker deciding this needs something concrete to
work with:

- **Travel**: appointments, therapy, contact arrangements. Mileage and how often.
- **Therapy or support** you are paying for privately, and why.
- **Equipment** and adaptations.
- **Respite**: what you need and what it costs.
- **Extra wear and tear**: replacement bedding, clothing, damage.
- **Lost earnings** where you have had to reduce your hours.

Where the need arises from what happened to the child before they came to you, say so plainly. That
is squarely within what the regulation covers, and it is often left out because carers assume it
only means physical disability.

## If you are turned down

Ask for the reasons in writing. Then ask what evidence would change the answer. A refusal that
cannot explain itself is worth taking to the [complaints
procedure](/guidance/complaining-to-the-ombudsman).
