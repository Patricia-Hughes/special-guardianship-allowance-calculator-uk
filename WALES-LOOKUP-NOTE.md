# One lookup for the researcher: two Welsh rate bulletins

Updated 1 September 2026. This is the only research job left, and it is optional.

## What we are after

The Welsh Government's own published table of National Minimum Allowances for
foster carers, for two years:

- **2024 to 2025**
- **2026 to 2027**

## Where they live

Welsh rates are not published as a normal web page. They go out as **bilingual
email bulletins**, produced by Care Inspectorate Wales on behalf of the Welsh
Government, and sent to councils and fostering agencies.

We already have the **2023 to 2024** one, which proves the format and the
address pattern:

```
https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/UKWALES_CSSIW_INT/bulletins/3450cef
```

Note the account name in that address, `UKWALES_CSSIW_INT`. Every one of these
bulletins lives under it. Only the code at the end changes.

Titles look like this, in Welsh first and then English:

> ISAFSWM LWFANSAU CENEDLAETHOL AR GYFER GOFALWYR MAETH 2023 - 2024 /
> NATIONAL MINIMUM ALLOWANCES FOSTER CARERS 2023 - 2024

## Three places to try, in order

1. **The Care Inspectorate Wales bulletin archive.** Same email system as the
   link above. Look for the same title with 2024-2025 and 2026-2027 in it.
2. **The National Archives UK Government Web Archive.** It keeps dated
   snapshots of `gov.wales` and `careinspectorate.wales`. Try snapshots from
   February and March of 2024 and of 2026, which is when these are normally
   issued.
3. **Ask Care Inspectorate Wales directly.** They send the bulletins, so they
   can send them again. This is often faster than searching.

## One thing to warn you about

**The rate table inside the bulletin is a picture, not text.** You cannot
search it, copy it or paste it. Somebody has to look at the image and type the
numbers out. So please send us either a screenshot or the four figures typed by
hand, and say which you did.

We need, for each year:

- 0 to 4 years, per week
- 5 to 15 years, per week
- 16 to 17 years, per week

## Do not be surprised by this

In the Welsh figures, **the youngest band is paid more than the middle band**.
For 2025/26 it was 224 pounds for a 0 to 4 year old and 204 pounds for a 5 to
15 year old. That looks like a typo and it is not. We have confirmed it across
two separate years. **Please transcribe exactly what is printed and do not
correct it.** If the years you find show the same pattern, that is expected.

## What happens if you cannot find them

Nothing breaks and nothing needs deciding.

Welsh carers already get their current figure, because we hold 2026/27 from
other sources. The only thing missing is the "what should I have been paid in
past years" feature, for Wales only. The site tells a Welsh carer plainly which
years it cannot cover and why, rather than guessing.

So please spend an hour on it, not a week. If it does not turn up, tell us and
we will leave it as an acknowledged gap.
